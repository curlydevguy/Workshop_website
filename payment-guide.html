SBI Collect Payment Guide

Follow these step-by-step instructions to pay your workshop registration fee securely through State Bank of India's SBI Collect portal.

Open SBI Collect Portal ↗ ← Back to Registration
1

Open the SBI Collect Portal

Visit the official SBI Collect website: www.onlinesbi.sbi/sbicollect.

On the homepage, you will see a search bar asking for the institution or corporate name.

2

Search for IIT Roorkee

In the search box, select Educational Institutions or type:

Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (or filter State: Uttarakhand → Type: Educational Institutions)

Click on Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee from the search results to proceed.

3

Select Payment Category

Under Payment Category, look for the dropdown menu and select the workshop:

Conference / Workshop / AI for Secure 6G: Foundations of FL, XAI and LLMs
4

Fill in Participant Details & Fee Amount

Enter your full name, email address, mobile number, and institution name matching what you entered in the workshop registration form.

Select your fee category:

  • Without Accommodation: ₹3,000
  • With Accommodation: ₹3,000 + ₹700 per night stayed (e.g. 5 nights 19–24 Dec = ₹6,500 total)
5

Complete Payment & Download Receipt

Choose your preferred payment method: UPI (GPay / PhonePe / Paytm), Net Banking, or Debit / Credit Card.

Crucial Step: After payment completes, SBI Collect generates an e-Receipt with a reference number starting with DU.... Take a clear screenshot or download the PDF receipt.
6

Submit Payment Proof on Workshop Website

Return to our Registration Page:

  1. Enter your SBI Collect reference number (e.g. DU12345678).
  2. Upload your confirmation screenshot or PDF.
  3. Click Submit Payment Proof to finish reserving your seat.
