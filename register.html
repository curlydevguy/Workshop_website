Instructions

How to register

  1. 1 Choose whether you need on-campus accommodation — if you do, pick your check-in and check-out dates and the fee updates live.
  2. 2 Fill in your details and click Submit Registration to reserve your seat.
  3. 3 A Pay via SBI Collect button will then appear — click it to open SBI's secure payment page in a new tab and complete your payment there.
  4. 4 Come back to this page, enter your SBI Collect reference number and payment date, and upload a screenshot of your payment confirmation to confirm your seat.
