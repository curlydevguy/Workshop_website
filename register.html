Instructions

How to register

  1. 1 Review the workshop details and fee structure to confirm your registration category.
  2. 2 Fill in the form with your details — the fee updates automatically based on your category.
  3. 3 Click Submit Registration to reserve your seat.
  4. 4 A Pay via SBI Collect button will then appear — click it to open SBI's secure payment page in a new tab and complete your payment there.
  5. 5 Come back to this page, enter your SBI Collect reference number and payment date, and upload a screenshot of your payment confirmation to confirm your seat.
