Workshop Overview

6G networks are shaping up to be smarter, more connected, and a lot more autonomous than anything before them but that same intelligence brings a much bigger attack surface with it, from the radio layer all the way up to the AI models now running inside the network itself. This SPARC sponsored workshop, hosted by IIT Roorkee, brings together researchers, practitioners, and students to dig into how federated learning, explainable AI, and large language models can actually be put to work catching threats early, making network intelligence trustworthy, and building security into 6G systems from the ground up.
