About the workshop

AI for Secure 6G: Foundations of FL, XAI, and LLMs

AI for Secure 6G: Foundations of FL, XAI, and LLMs is a SPARC Sponsored Lab, Indo-international hybrid workshop examining how artificial intelligence can be used to secure 6G networks — and the new risks AI itself introduces into them. The program moves from foundational concepts to applied, hands-on sessions across three days, combining keynote talks, technical deep-dives, a hands-on lab, and open discussion with speakers from India and abroad.

The workshop is organized under the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Roorkee, and is open to students, faculty, and industry professionals working at the intersection of networking, security, and machine learning.

Focus areas

What the sessions cover

  • Federated Learning for 6G — privacy-preserving, distributed model training across network edges and devices
  • Explainable AI (XAI) — making AI-driven security decisions interpretable and auditable
  • LLM Security — risks and defenses for large language models used in network operations and tooling
  • Adversarial Machine Learning — attack and defense techniques for ML models deployed in wireless networks
  • Secure Network Slicing — AI-assisted policy enforcement across isolated network slices
Objectives

What participants will gain

  • A working understanding of the 6G threat landscape and where AI fits into both attack and defense
  • Hands-on exposure to building federated, privacy-preserving security models
  • Familiarity with explainability methods for network-security AI systems
  • Awareness of emerging LLM-specific risks in network operations
  • Direct engagement with international and Indian researchers through talks and discussion
